Privacy policy

Chat Losing It Clipper · Last updated 19 November 2026

Chat Losing It Clipper ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Chat Losing It team to upload short clips to our own social media channels. This policy explains what the app does with data from Google and other platforms.

Who uses the app

Only the Chat Losing It team. The app runs on our own computer and is not offered to the public. Nobody else signs in to it or connects their account to it.

Google and YouTube data

When we connect our own YouTube channel, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload), used only to upload clips we have approved to our own channel;
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), used only to show which channel is connected and the status of uploaded clips.

The access token Google gives us is stored only on our own computer. It is never sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. We don't use it to read, change or delete anything other than the clips the app uploads.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Uploads to YouTube are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other people's data

The app does not collect personal data about viewers. To find highlights, it may read the public chat of a livestream while recording. It keeps only short samples next to a clip so we can review it, and it deletes the recording buffer as it goes.

Removing access

We can disconnect the app at any time from the app itself, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When the app is disconnected, the stored token is deleted.
